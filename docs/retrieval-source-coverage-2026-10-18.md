## Focused candidate

`workspace-source-coverage-v1` starts with unchanged hybrid retrieval and complete-parent restoration, then adds at most three question-relevant listing sources. It preserves every baseline parent, its order, full text and original customer question. The same eight-parent, 1,500-character-per-parent and 12,000-source-character bounds apply. Supplemented sources have no invented hybrid ranks and carry explicit selection reasons.

Rules cover requested structured dimensions, color, material and capacity; product/model/part identity; and literal identifier or bare-family listing context. Item and package dimensions are distinct. Model punctuation remains significant. A family mention selects context and does not establish compatibility. The vocabulary is deliberately narrow and recognizes a source's first structured key; unrelated attributes are not added.

The candidate is available through an internal workspace constructor option; the current default and answer instructions remain unchanged. An injected actual-workspace request/receipt/restart test confirms that added bodies reach generation without redispatch on recovery.

On the exposed development examples, pure assembly rescues the omitted color field, Toyota dimensions/model number and Viking part/family identifiers. This establishes source delivery, not improved answer quality.
