It preserves every baseline parent, its order, full text and original customer question. The same eight-parent, 1,500-character-per-parent and 12,000-source-character bounds apply. Supplemented sources have no invented hybrid ranks and carry explicit selection reasons.

Rules cover requested structured dimensions, color, material and capacity; product/model/part identity; and literal identifier or bare-family listing context. Item and package dimensions are distinct. Model punctuation remains significant. A family mention selects context and does not establish compatibility. Requested clauses drive every facet: a declarative background such as “I already know the dimensions” does not consume slots needed for requested color, material and capacity. Dimension scopes resolve within each requested clause. The vocabulary and clause heuristic are deliberately bounded and recognize a source's first structured key; this is not a semantic intent guarantee, and candidate misses remain in the experiment.

The candidate is available through an internal workspace constructor option; the current default and answer instructions remain unchanged. An injected actual-workspace request/receipt/restart test confirms that added bodies reach generation without redispatch on recovery.

On the exposed development examples, pure assembly rescues the omitted color field, Toyota dimensions/model number and Viking part/family identifiers. This establishes source delivery, not improved answer quality.
