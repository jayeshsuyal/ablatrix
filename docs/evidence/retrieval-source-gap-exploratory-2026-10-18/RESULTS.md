# Source coverage v1: stopped before paid testing

October 18, 2026. **No useful missing fact was recovered in the three identified gap cases. No answer-quality improvement was measured.** The default retrieval remains active.

## What ran

The [frozen exploratory protocol](selection-protocol-v2.json) retained the entire ordered 44-product diagnostic prefix from the failed 64-product design: 862 saved source bodies. Baseline retrieval produced five full parent sources per product. Independent AI reviewers then checked all 44 questions against their complete source packets, titles, original CQA scopes and baseline contexts. Candidate contexts and generated answers were withheld during source review.

After preserving that failed outcome, a separate [offline diagnostic](offline-retrieval-diagnostic-v1.json) applied the unchanged `workspace-source-coverage-v1` candidate to all 44 baseline contexts. It changed **10/44 contexts**, while delivering **0/4 designated missing-fact units across 0/3 gap cases**. All baseline parents are retained by the additive candidate contract. Context growth did not supply the useful missing facts.

## What this establishes

This is a negative source-delivery result on a source-exposed exploratory pool. It establishes neither general failure prevalence nor answer quality. **Zero project provider calls and zero answers were generated; saved human judgments remain zero.** Literal citation matching, semantic citation support, answer correctness and human-reviewed quality were not measured in this phase. No latency or cost reduction was measured.

The [stop record](stop-record-v1.json) binds the raw artifact hashes and failed eligibility. Prior live results remain separate in the [source-calibrated answer comparison](../answer-quality-source-calibration-2026-10-18/RESULTS.md).
